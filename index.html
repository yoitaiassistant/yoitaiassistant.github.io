Yo

A private personal assistant

Yo is a private, single-user AI assistant that helps its owner manage their calendar, tasks, habits, notes and email. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Yo connects to its own Google account (Gmail and Google Calendar) and to calendars its owner has explicitly shared with it. It only acts on instructions from its owner.
