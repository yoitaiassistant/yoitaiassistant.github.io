Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Yo is a private personal assistant used by a single person (its owner). It is not available to the public.

What data Yo accesses

  • Email in Yo's own Gmail account.
  • Events in Yo's own Google Calendar and in calendars its owner has explicitly shared with it.
  • Files in Yo's own Google Drive, Docs and Sheets, and its own contacts.

How the data is used

Only to perform tasks its owner asks for: managing the calendar, tasks and habits, preparing briefs and summaries, and drafting messages. Data is processed by an AI model provider solely to carry out those tasks.

Storage and sharing

  • Access tokens and notes are stored only on the owner's private computer.
  • Data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties beyond what is needed to perform the owner's tasks.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removal

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
